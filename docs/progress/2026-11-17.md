## What happened

**The full pipeline at full scale** (`docs/experiments/06_full_pipeline_and_labels.md`, Part 2). All 52
session-cameras plus one augmented copy, the tuned detector (D3) and tracker (T2, BoT-SORT + ReID +
CMC), per-labeler models evaluated per rating entry, leaving one group out. The model predicts 3 for
96% of expert entries: AUC 0.527, Spearman 0.072, MAE 0.674 against 0.619 for always predicting 3.
It underfits (training AUC 0.57), the same with logistic regression, a random forest or k-NN (an SVM
was dropped as impractically slow, about 1.5 hours per arm), and one model pooled over all labelers
does as well as one per labeler.

**The labels** (Part 1, `scripts/label_agreement.py`). Krippendorff's α is between −0.06 and +0.04 on
every unit (entry moments, seconds, windows) and every interpolation. Predicted from the other
labelers, on the model's own entries and metrics, a labeler's entry reaches AUC 0.534 (one other
expert) to 0.564 (the other experts' mean), and **no human combination beats constant 3 on MAE**.
Labelers are not more consistent with themselves than with each other. The model is about half-way
between chance and that ceiling.

**Augmentation** (experiment 06 §2.3). One augmented copy changes nothing at full scale (AUC 0.527
against 0.525 without); heavier augmentation is not pursued, since the model underfits.

## What FYP-1 can claim

1. **Simple, cheap features can do well enough** — to be shown, against the human ceiling rather than
   constant 3. The current features close about half the gap; the clustering-based features are the
   test.
2. **A single number cannot score attention** — supported by the label analysis, developed in FYP-2:
   labelers disagree on the moment's rating but differ systematically in level and style, and the
   shared signal sits at the session level. FYP-2 replaces the single rating with a suite of
   statistics.

Short term, evaluation stays perspectivist (each labeler scored separately, never an aggregated gold);
long term, the project should move to labels humans agree on, on DIPSER (session-level, self-report)
or another dataset, checked with `label_agreement` before training.
